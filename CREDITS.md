*Which Way Is Up?* was created by **Olli “Hectigo” Etuaho**, copyright 2007.
This independent refresh is directed and maintained by **Mohtab Arabiat**, with AI
coding assistance. It is an homage and adaptation of the original game.

## A Linux memory

“Which Way Is Up? was the first game I tried when I started using Linux. This
refresh is my way of returning to that first experience: keeping its playful
spirit alive, and making room for new players, faster runs and worlds of their
own.” — Mohtab Arabiat
